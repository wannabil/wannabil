## Hi, I'm Nabil 👋

Self-taught full-stack developer from the Klang Valley, Malaysia. I run **[Miracle Mikhael](https://miraclemikhael.com)**, a small studio that builds websites and business systems for Malaysian SMEs. Lately most of that work has AI agents built in: they reply to customers, chase payments, send reminders and put together daily reports, and they check with the owner before anything important.

My bio says "I build everyday" and I mean it. Client work during the day, side projects and odd experiments at night.

### What I'm working on

- 🚗 **[JomSewa](https://getjomsewa.com)**: booking and fleet system for Malaysian car rental businesses. Customers book through a link, upload IC and licence, and sign the agreement online.
- 🗣️ **[KawanCakap](https://github.com/wannabil/kawancakap)**: free AI speaking partner for SPM English Speaking Part 3, with band feedback and a teacher dashboard.
- 🤖 **AI agent workflows**: skills, hooks and automations that let agents handle real day-to-day business admin.
